# Hypertime: order and branching without an outside clock

**Status, 2026-09-28.** [HYPER] Tyler Roost describes universempiternity
as appearing to contain base realities like **hyperpheres** on an apparent
two-dimensional grid, with Hyperspace and Hypertime as guessed axes. This
repository investigates the second axis. “Appearing” is preserved: no
physical observer, embedding, dimension, or completed map is established.

## A display index is not duration

[FRAME] Let `P` be a finite set of stable reality-presentation identifiers
and `h_t: P → ℤ` an integer display index. `ℤ` is the set of integers;
the index, identifiers, and the ordering relation are dimensionless. No
seconds, rate, external clock, absolute date, or observer-independent
simultaneity is inferred. Several presentations may share one index while
remaining distinct.

Let `A ⊆ P × P` be a declared advance relation. Every `(a,b) ∈ A` must
satisfy `h_t(a) < h_t(b)`. The strict inequality forbids a directed cycle
within this finite [FRAME], but does not prohibit self-reference or loops
in Tyler's larger ontology. `declared_precedes(a,b)` means that a nonempty
finite path of `A` edges leads from `a` to `b`. The module does not infer an
edge from `h_t(a) < h_t(b)`, nor call an edge physical causation, creation,
travel, or a theorem about time.

In the negative control, `origin` has declared advances to `branch-a` and
`branch-b`, both at index 1. Neither branch precedes the other. A separate
presentation at index 5 is not reachable merely because its number is
larger. A reverse or tied-index advance is rejected. Thus the grid's
vertical placement is weaker than an actual relation among realities.

## Relation to other time and reality senses

User-stated base-reality has internal temporal life; sempiternity and
universempiternity were described as atemporal. A Hypertime index here
orders a **presentation of relations among base-realities**, not moments
inside one base-reality or moments of an atemporal whole. [OPEN] Whether
this display axis can be interpreted as branching order, relative
construction order, observer chronology, another order, or no intrinsic
order at all requires a native rule and an observation contract. If the
axis turns out to be only a drawing coordinate, its name must not be used
to smuggle in a clock.

Tyler's statement “Realities are that which are observable” belongs to the
new joint research frame. Hyperspace tests declared channels and bound
records; Hypertime does not infer observability from precedence. The
[Hyperstratum canonical definition](https://github.com/TimeLordRaps/hyperstratum/blob/ecf972f289d6e269e302638856227b3e3f85ac6d/specs/canonical-definitions.md)
of Reality as a situated slice remains a separate prior definition, with
its relation to the new criterion [OPEN].
